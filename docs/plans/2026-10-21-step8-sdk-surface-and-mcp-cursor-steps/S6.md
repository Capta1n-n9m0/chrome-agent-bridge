### 2026-10-23 · `bb-login.ps1`'s post-submit wait becomes condition-based (proposed, not built)
**Decided (proposed):** after its one press, `bb-login.ps1` polls (for example every 1 s, up to a bound such as
90 s) until all three hold:
- the address-bar host is `ada.blackboard.com`;
- no sign-in control remains;
- the address bar has been stable for at least 4 s.

This replaces today's fixed `Start-Sleep -Seconds 3` and then `Wait-Settled` (a 4-s-stable address bar within
`-SettleSec 20`). Exit 4 comes only when the bound expires. It is still one press, with no reading of fields.
Before any live use, dry-run it on the localhost stand-ins with a delayed redirect:
- a known-good slow page (redirects after a delay inside the bound) ⇒ exit 0;
- a page that never returns ⇒ exit 4 at the bound, with the one press only.

**Why:** Part 3's exit 4 was a check made too early (#101): the user reports the SSO login worked, and the script
read `after submit: on ada.blackboard.com: no; sign-in buttons: 1` about 8 s after the press. A condition with a
bound waits as long as the round trip needs and no longer.
**Instead of:** a longer fixed sleep, which is still a guess; a second press, which is outside #96/#99's one UI
press; or dropping `bb-login.ps1` and relying on a login the user made by hand.
**Revisit if:** the SSO adds a step (MFA, a consent page), or the round trip outlasts the bound.
**By:** Claude (S6 step agent), for the session agent.
