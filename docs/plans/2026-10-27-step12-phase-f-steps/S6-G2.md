## 5. Proposed decisions

### 2026-10-28 · S6-G2: a client's own `focusTab` before a trusted input stays harmless; the daemon focuses again under its hold
**Decided:** the SDK's and MCP's `focusFirst` (#89, #106) keep sending `focusTab` first; that call takes and frees the lock
as before, and the trusted input then focuses again under its own hold. The extension's `focusTab` (`tabs.get`, effect
check, `noteFocus`, `windows.update {focused}`, `tabs.update {active}`) is idempotent on a tab already in front; there is
no re-entrancy, so no deadlock; on success the answer is the input's. Cost: one extra round trip, and a caller's next
lock-taking call waits out its own pause (≤ 500 ms). **Why:** the focus under the hold is the one that closes the race,
and the lane allows no SDK/MCP change. **Instead of:** dropping the clients' own `focusTab` (an SDK/MCP slice). **Revisit
if:** the latency matters; dropping it is then a separate optional slice. **By:** Claude (proposed)

### 2026-10-28 · S6-G2: no input after a failed or late focus; the pause after every input outcome
**Decided:** a failed daemon `focusTab` answers with its error; a disconnect or a lease loss after the wait or between the
forwards answers `NOT_CONNECTED`/`TAB_HELD`/`TAB_GONE`; a focus settling at or past the deadline answers `TIMEOUT`. In
each case no input is forwarded and the lock is released at once. After the input forward, on any outcome, the lock is
held `min(500, deadline − now)` after the reply. **Why:** never send input to an unfocused tab or past the caller's
deadline; nothing to settle when nothing was sent. **Instead of:** forwarding the input anyway. **Revisit if:** a raw
client needs the input's own message where `focusTab`'s refusal now answers (same codes, both class `tab` in the gate).
**By:** Claude (proposed, contract-level, for user review)

### 2026-10-28 · S6-G2: a raw trusted input now brings its tab to the front
**Decided:** stated in the §6.4 note: a trusted input sent without its own `focusTab` (a raw client, `recipes/fe.ts`,
`recipes/t5.ts`, the SDK's `"none"` policy) is now focused first, as `focusFirst` does; before, a background tab got the
input unfocused (Run 8 BG rows). This is #338's rule, not a new choice; recorded so it is visible. Stale for S8's doc pass:
`sdk/src/policy.ts:36` and `mcp/CLAUDE.md:54` (the F19 "two calls" sentence). **By:** Claude (proposed, for user review)
