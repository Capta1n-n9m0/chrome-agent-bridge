once with no opener, as before S1. **Why:** #385 says a tab without `openerTabId` is sent at once, and keeping today's
output is the conservative side; no such case was measured (60 of 60 joined tabs had an opener, Stage 0). **Instead
of:** granting from `sourceTabId` alone. **Revisit if:** Run 20 finds a popup with a source but no Chrome opener.
